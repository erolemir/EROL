# Seo Content Brief

Create useful search-oriented content briefs from audience questions, original evidence and clear page intent.

## Workflow

1. Define the audience question and intended page outcome; use current permitted search evidence rather than invented keyword volumes.
2. Identify original expertise, demonstrations or data that the page can contribute. Distinguish factual inputs from claims needing verification.
3. Outline an answer structure, title intent, supporting questions and relevant internal links without arbitrary word-count or repetition targets.
4. Keep author/source accountability and readable explanations; do not manufacture credentials, testimonials or answers the evidence cannot support.

## Verification

Review whether the brief resolves the audience question and avoids unsupported claims; later indexing/ranking outcomes need separate observation.

## Deliverable

Audience/search intent, evidence gaps and an original content outline.

## Current reference

Verify platform-specific details against [official documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) for the deployed version.
