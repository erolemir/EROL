# Sales Pipeline Design

Design a sales pipeline around buyer evidence, stage exit criteria and reliable operational reporting.

## Workflow

1. Define buyer journey, deal unit and source of opportunity evidence; avoid stages based only on seller activity.
2. Specify entry/exit criteria, owner, next action and aging limits for each stage; distinguish unqualified interest from forecastable opportunity.
3. Separate pipeline coverage, win rate, cycle time and realized revenue using consistent cohort/period definitions.
4. Draft CRM fields and follow-up playbooks while retaining explicit authorization for outbound contact or CRM mutations.

## Verification

Apply stages to sample deals, inspect ambiguous cases and reconcile forecasts to actual qualification evidence.

## Deliverable

Stage map, qualification checklist and measurement definitions.
