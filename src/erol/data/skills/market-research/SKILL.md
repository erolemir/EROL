# Market Research

Research a market and competitors with dated evidence, explicit segments and defensible comparisons.

## Workflow

1. Define geography, customer segment, buying job and decision before researching; distinguish direct competitors from substitutes.
2. Collect current primary pricing/product evidence and dated customer observations; record source limits and avoid invented market-size numbers.
3. Compare on buyer-relevant criteria with consistent units and assumptions, including switching costs and distribution constraints.
4. Separate observations, estimates and hypotheses; show what evidence would change the recommended market entry decision.

## Verification

Check each material factual claim against its source and reconcile sizing assumptions; flag missing or noncomparable data.

## Deliverable

Source-linked comparison, opportunity hypotheses and validation questions.
