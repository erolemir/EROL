# Customer Discovery

Plan unbiased customer discovery that separates observed problems from reactions to a proposed solution.

## Workflow

1. State the uncertain customer/problem hypothesis and recruiting criteria; avoid selecting only enthusiastic supporters.
2. Ask about concrete recent behavior, workarounds, consequences and buying context before presenting the product.
3. Use neutral prompts and capture evidence with consent; separate respondent words, analyst interpretation and untested claims.
4. Look for repeated patterns and contradictions across the sample, then choose the next validation step rather than declaring market fit from compliments.

## Verification

Review questions for leading assumptions and verify conclusions are traceable to actual observations. Do not fabricate interviews or quotes.

## Deliverable

Interview guide, sampling plan and evidence synthesis template; contacting people requires explicit authorization.
