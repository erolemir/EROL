# Customer Segmentation

Define actionable customer segments from needs, behavior and reachable evidence rather than invented personas.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the decision the segmentation will support and use permitted aggregated/customer data with known coverage.
2. Group by meaningful needs, context or behavior that changes the product/message; avoid stereotypes and unsupported demographic assumptions.
3. Define inclusion rules, reachable channels and a measurable segment outcome; distinguish segments from creative personas.
4. Check segment size, stability and overlap, then describe how new evidence can revise boundaries.

## Verification

Apply rules to a sample, inspect ambiguous accounts and verify each segment leads to a distinct testable action.

## Deliverable

Segment definitions, evidence quality and prioritized validation actions.
