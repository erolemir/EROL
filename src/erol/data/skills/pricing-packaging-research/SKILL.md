# Pricing Packaging Research

Research product pricing and packaging from customer value, unit economics and defensible willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Workflow

1. Define buyer, value unit and cost drivers; separate price level, usage metric, feature packaging and billing conditions.
2. Collect dated competitor and customer evidence without copying their tiers as proof of fit; disclose sampling and incentive biases.
3. Model scenarios with explicit demand, support and cost assumptions; keep estimates distinct from observed willingness to pay.
4. Propose reversible validation and grandfathering/communication considerations; do not alter live prices or billing without authorization.

## Verification

Check arithmetic, entitlement boundaries and evidence for claimed value; define how interviews or an authorized price test would falsify the proposal.

## Deliverable

Pricing hypotheses, scenario model and validation/rollout questions.
