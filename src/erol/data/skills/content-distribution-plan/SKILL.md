# Content Distribution Plan

Plan reusable content and channel distribution around audience needs, capacity and measurable outcomes.

## Workflow

1. Choose audience questions and channel objectives; inventory owned assets, production capacity and current evidence.
2. Plan a few substantive source pieces and channel-specific adaptations without mass-producing duplicate or unsupported content.
3. Specify owner, format, distribution context, call to action and tracking definition for each useful item.
4. Keep publication and outreach within explicit authorization; revise cadence based on audience response and actual delivery capacity.

## Verification

Check factual consistency across adaptations, link/tracking readiness and capacity; measure outcome quality rather than post count alone.

## Deliverable

Capacity-aware calendar, distribution briefs and measurement plan.
