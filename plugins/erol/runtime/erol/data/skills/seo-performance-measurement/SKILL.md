# Seo Performance Measurement

Measure organic-search outcomes using comparable query/page cohorts and explicit attribution limits.

## Workflow

1. Define the business outcome and dated baseline; separate search visibility, visits and downstream conversions.
2. Compare equivalent page/query/device/region cohorts and note aggregation or privacy gaps in available data.
3. Check seasonality, brand demand, tracking changes and concurrent releases before assigning an effect to an SEO change.
4. Report uncertainty and alternative explanations; ranking or click movement alone does not prove incremental business impact.

## Verification

Reconcile exported totals and denominators, retain query/filter definitions and check the result against an unchanged comparison group where available.

## Deliverable

Reproducible search report, attribution limits and next measurement decision.
