# Brand Positioning

Develop positioning from customer alternatives, credible differentiation and proof.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience, job, existing alternative and purchase context before choosing a category or slogan.
2. Connect differentiation to a customer outcome with available proof; separate shipped capabilities from roadmap promises.
3. Write a concise positioning statement and message hierarchy using the buyer's language, with explicit tradeoffs and exclusions.
4. Prepare contrasting variants for validation; avoid claiming universal leadership or guaranteed results without evidence.

## Verification

Check factual claims against the product and test comprehension with representative feedback when available; label untested positioning as a hypothesis.

## Deliverable

Positioning statement, proof points and message-test plan.
