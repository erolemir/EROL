# Paid Social Campaign

Prepare paid-social creative tests with credible messages, audience boundaries and budget-aware measurement.

## Workflow

1. Clarify outcome, allowed audiences, placements and authorized spend; use current platform policies and observed account capabilities before selecting settings.
2. Design genuinely different creative hypotheses around audience needs with truthful product demonstrations and offer conditions.
3. Keep audience overlap, attribution windows and tracking consent explicit; do not treat platform-attributed conversions as proven incrementality.
4. Set bounded tests and stop rules; keep launch, budget changes and uploads within explicit authorization.

## Verification

Review creative claims, destination consistency, event deduplication and budget caps; compare equivalent cohorts and disclose small-sample uncertainty.

## Deliverable

Creative briefs, campaign draft and budget/measurement guardrails.
