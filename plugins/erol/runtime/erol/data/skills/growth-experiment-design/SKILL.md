# Growth Experiment Design

Design bounded business growth experiments with falsifiable hypotheses, guardrails and decision rules.

## Workflow

1. Identify the bottleneck and a falsifiable mechanism; distinguish acquisition, activation, retention and revenue outcomes.
2. Choose one primary outcome, eligibility rules, baseline and guardrail metrics before selecting tactics.
3. Set exposure, time/spend boundaries and a decision rule that accounts for sample size and attribution limits.
4. Prioritize experiments by evidence and learning value, not unsupported guaranteed growth forecasts; keep external actions within task authorization.

## Verification

Audit event definitions, compare eligible cohorts and check guardrails; separate a directional signal from a conclusive causal result.

## Deliverable

Experiment brief, measurement plan and continue/stop decision criteria.
