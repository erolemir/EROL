# Experiment Statistics

Evaluate experiments with valid randomization, uncertainty and predeclared outcome decisions.

## Workflow

1. Confirm assignment unit, eligibility, exposure and primary outcome before looking at significance; inspect randomization and sample balance.
2. Use an analysis matching the metric and design, with effect size and uncertainty rather than a p-value alone.
3. Account for repeated users, clustering, missing outcomes, multiple comparisons and the chosen stopping/peeking method.
4. Check practical importance and guardrail outcomes; distinguish insufficient evidence from proof of no effect.

## Verification

Reconcile assigned/exposed cohorts, run sanity checks and show reproducible calculations on the permitted data with method assumptions disclosed.

## Deliverable

Experiment quality assessment, effect estimate/interval and defensible decision.
