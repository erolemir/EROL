# Partnership Strategy

Evaluate partnerships from mutual customer value, economics, responsibilities and pilot evidence.

## Workflow

1. Identify the shared customer problem and why both sides can deliver value unavailable through existing channels.
2. Evaluate audience overlap, economics, integration/support effort and ownership of customer relationships using dated evidence.
3. Define a bounded pilot with attribution, responsibilities, data-access limits and a clear exit path.
4. Separate an outreach/proposal draft from an agreement; do not contact partners or commit commercial terms without explicit authorization.

## Verification

Check assumptions, cost/revenue arithmetic and pilot metrics; list unresolved commercial/legal questions without claiming a contract review.

## Deliverable

Partner shortlist rationale, pilot brief and go/no-go evidence requirements.
